Hi there!

I am Arvinth Shanmugam, a Post-Baccalaureate Research Fellow at the Wadhwani School of Data Science & AI, IIT Madras. I hold an Integrated B.S. & M.S. in Biological Sciences (Specialization in Computational Biology) from IIT Madras (Class Rank: 4, GPA: 8.77/10).

My research sits at the intersection of mathematical modeling, machine learning, and public health — with a focus on infectious disease dynamics, cancer burden estimation, and intervention policy optimization for low- and middle-income country settings.

Currently, I am:

  • Building spatiotemporal models for HPV/cervical cancer burden across Indian states.
  • Developing ML frameworks for disease burden prediction in data-limited settings.
  • Working on cost-effectiveness analyses for opportunistic cancer screening programs.
