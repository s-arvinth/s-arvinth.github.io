Hello, I am
Arvinth a Mathematical Modeler, Computational Biologist and a Data Scientist an animal lover

Hi there!

I build mathematical and AI models of how disease spreads and what it takes to stop it.

I'm currently a Post-Baccalaureate Research Fellow at the Wadhwani School of Data Science & AI, IIT Madras, advised by Prof. Nirav Bhatt. I am a graduate of IIT Madras, with an Integrated B.S. & M.S. in Biological Sciences.

I currently work with ICMR on HPV and Cervical Cancer. As a disease modeler, I build age-region-structured transmission models for HPV and cervical cancer to simulate combined intervention policy scenarios. My work focuses on targeting the ideal age and population and devising optimal intervention strategies for cervical cancer elimination in India. As a data scientist, I also build learning-based models for global disease burden estimation and longitudinal forecasting.
