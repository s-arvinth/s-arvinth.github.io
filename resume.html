Select Research Projects

Modeling and Control of Epidemiological Processes for HPV Spread and Cervical Cancer NDMC IITB

Advisor: Prof. Nirav Bhatt

  • Developed and calibrated age-structured compartmental models for HPV transmission and cervical carcinogenesis, aligned with Indian epidemiological baselines.
  • Extended the framework to integrate age-targeted HPV vaccination and population screening, enabling impact and evaluation of combined intervention policy scenarios.
  • Conducted simulations quantifying the effects of varying vaccination/screening coverage, target age bands, and introduction timing on infections, precancerous lesions, cancer cases, and mortality.
  • Generated State-Level Population Projections data using Bayesian hierarchical modeling & Logistic-window functions.
  • Built a decision analytic framework by solving an optimal-control problem to allocate time-varying vaccination and screening intensities under resource constraints, minimizing long-run cervical-cancer burden.

Predicting Global HPV and CIN Burden from Limited and Missing Data using Machine Learning NDMC IITB

Advisor: Prof. Nirav Bhatt

  • Curated multi-country epidemiologic, sociodemographic & health system covariates; engineered STI-burden and disease-incidence indices; engineered composite risk indices and applied imputation to address missingness.
  • Built and cross-validated machine-learning models on imbalanced targets using data rebalancing using SMOGN.
  • Predicted the Global and Indian state-level disease burden estimates with diagnostic analyses.

Agent-Based Modeling with HPVsim for State-Level HPV/Cervical-Cancer Dynamics NDMC IITB

Advisor: Prof. Nirav Bhatt

  • Extended HPVsim with maintained patches to enable subnational stratification with state-level demographic and epidemiological inputs.
  • Performed state-level microsimulations for India incorporating variation in sexual behavior and demographic patterns.

Longitudinal Growth and Malnutrition Dynamics in Early Childhood: AMANHI-ACT Cohorts CPHK

Advisors: Dr. Sunil Sazawal, Prof. Nirav Bhatt

  • SITAR Growth-Curve Modeling (Bangladesh, Pakistan, Tanzania).
    • Fitted nonlinear mixed-effects SITAR models for height and weight, extracting size, timing, and intensity parameters with subgroup stratification (sex, SGA/AGA, birth-weight classes).
    • Generated mean and velocity curves and random-effects correlation maps to characterize cohort-specific growth trajectories and identify early growth faltering signals.
  • Multi-State Markov Modeling of Malnutrition Transitions.
    • Defined seven nutritional states (Normal; Underweight, Stunted, Wasted; and compounded states) and estimated continuous-time transition intensities and one-year transition probabilities from cohort follow-ups.
    • Quantified Median First Passage/Recurrence Times and lifetime time-in-state to reveal persistence of stunting and rapid cycling among compounded deficits, versus relatively faster recovery from wasting.

Cost-Effectiveness Analysis using Markov Modeling for Opportunistic Prostate Cancer Screening Apollo

Advisors: Dr. Srinivas Chilukuri, Prof. Nirav Bhatt

  • Developed a discrete-time cohort Markov model of prostate-cancer natural history and screening pathways, parameterized with urban Indian epidemiology over a 20-year horizon.
  • Performed cost-effectiveness analysis from a health-system perspective, estimating costs and QALYs and reporting the incremental cost-effectiveness ratio (ICER) versus no screening.
  • Conducted uncertainty analysis (deterministic and probabilistic) and scenario testing to assess robustness of clinical and economic outcomes.
